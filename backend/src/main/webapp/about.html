Our Mission

Bridging Rescues with Loving Homes

PawsConnect was built to eliminate fragmentation in animal welfare by unifying shelters, ethical guidelines, and compassionate adopters.

Why We Exist

Traditional pet adoption often suffers from paperwork bottlenecks, unverified third-party classifieds, and lack of direct communication between shelter staff and adopters.

Our platform provides an integrated Java backend built with Jakarta Servlets, JDBC, and MySQL ensuring absolute transactional integrity and role-based data security.

🛡️

Platform Principles

  • ✔️ 100% Verified Shelters: Every listing is submitted by a registered organization.
  • ✔️ Administrative Moderation: Every animal listing is reviewed before public display.
  • ✔️ Zero Commercial Breeding: Focused strictly on rescue and adoption.
  • ✔️ Atomic Adoptions: Race conditions are prevented via ACID database transactions.
